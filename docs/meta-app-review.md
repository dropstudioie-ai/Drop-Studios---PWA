## General answers

**What does your app do?**
Thamires is the customer-service assistant for Drop Studios, a dance studio in Dublin, Ireland. It is used only by Drop Studios staff on Drop Studios' own Instagram professional account (@atdropstudios). When customers send Drop a direct message, comment on Drop's posts or tag Drop, the app drafts a helpful reply (class times, prices, booking links). A staff member reviews and approves replies in Drop's private admin page before they are sent. The app also helps staff publish a daily Story with the day's class timetable. It does not serve any other business.

## Per permission (request Advanced Access for each)

### instagram_basic
We use instagram_basic to read Drop Studios' own Instagram professional account profile (account ID and username) and the usernames of people who message or comment, so staff can see who they are replying to in our admin page. This is required by the other Instagram permissions we use.

### instagram_manage_messages
Customers send direct messages to @atdropstudios asking about class times, prices and how to book. We use instagram_manage_messages to receive these messages through webhooks and to send replies from @atdropstudios. Each reply is drafted by our assistant and approved by a Drop Studios staff member in our admin page before it is sent (staff can also enable automatic replies for simple timetable questions). Replies are only sent within the 24-hour messaging window. Without this permission customers would wait hours for answers about classes.

### instagram_manage_comments
People comment on Drop Studios' posts with questions ("what time is this class?") and tag @atdropstudios in their own posts after classes. We use instagram_manage_comments to receive comment and mention webhooks for our own account and to post a short public reply or thank-you from @atdropstudios after a staff member approves it. Complaints and sensitive topics are always handled by a person.

### instagram_content_publish
Every day Drop Studios posts a Story with that day's class timetable. Our admin page generates the Story image from our booking system, a staff member checks it and taps "Publish", and we use instagram_content_publish to publish it to @atdropstudios.

### instagram_manage_insights (only if it is listed)
We read the reach and replies of Drop Studios' own Stories (story_insights webhook) to show staff in a daily report how the timetable Stories perform.
